WARDEN privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026.

WARDEN is a private, single-user program. The only Google account that connects to it is its owner's.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, WARDEN reads motion events, still images and live video from the owner's own Google Nest cameras through Google's Smart Device Management API, and reads those events from a Google Cloud Pub/Sub subscription in the owner's own Google Cloud project.

How the data is used

Camera images are used to recognize wildlife on the owner's property and to operate the owner's own equipment in response. To recognize an animal, an image may be sent to an AI model provider for classification. The data is used for nothing else.

Storage and sharing

Images and event records are stored on a computer the owner controls. They are not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone, other than the classification step described above.

Other people

WARDEN has no other users and collects no information from visitors to this page. This page sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Removing access

The owner can revoke WARDEN's access at any time in their Google Account under third-party connections, and can delete the stored images and records from their own computer.
